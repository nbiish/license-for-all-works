## Case Law — Tribal Sovereignty & Jurisdiction

| Case | Citation | Holding | Status |
|------|----------|---------|--------|
| *Worcester v. Georgia* | 31 U.S. 515 (1832) | Tribes are "distinct, independent political communities, retaining their original natural rights." State law inapplicable in Indian Country. | Good law — foundational |
| *United States v. Wheeler* | 435 U.S. 313 (1978) | Tribal sovereignty is inherent, not delegated. Tribes retain "those aspects of sovereignty not withdrawn by treaty or statute." | Good law — foundational |
| *Winters v. United States* | 207 U.S. 564 (1908) | Reserved rights doctrine — tribes reserved all rights not explicitly ceded in treaties. | Good law — foundational |
| *County of Oneida v. Oneida Indian Nation* | 470 U.S. 226 (1985) | Indian canons of construction — treaty ambiguities resolved in favor of tribes as understood at time of signing. | Good law |
| *Menominee Tribe v. United States* | 391 U.S. 404 (1968) | Treaty rights capable of evolution and adaptation to modern circumstances. | Good law |
| *Santa Clara Pueblo v. Martinez* | 436 U.S. 49 (1978) | Inherent sovereignty not dependent on federal recognition; tribal self-governance. | Good law |
| *Montana v. United States* | 450 U.S. 544 (1981) | Establishes exceptions for tribal civil jurisdiction over non-Indians on fee land. | Good law — *Montana* exceptions apply |
| *Williams v. Lee* | 358 U.S. 217 (1959) | State courts lack jurisdiction over civil matters arising in Indian Country. | Good law |
| *National Farmers Union v. Crow Tribe* | 471 U.S. 845 (1985) | Tribal court primacy — must exhaust tribal remedies before federal court. | Good law |
| *Iowa Mutual Ins. v. LaPlante* | 480 U.S. 9 (1987) | Reinforces tribal court exhaustion requirement. | Good law |
| *White Mountain Apache Tribe v. Bracker* | 448 U.S. 136 (1980) | *Bracker* balancing test for federal preemption of state law in Indian Country. | Good law |
| *Washington v. Wash. State Commercial Passenger Fishing Vessel Ass'n* | 443 U.S. 658 (1979) | Treaty fishing rights — tribes reserved rights not explicitly ceded. | Good law |
| *United States v. Michigan* | 471 F. Supp. 192 (W.D. Mich. 1979) | 1836 Treaty fishing rights are "communal property of the signatory tribes." | Good law — specific to 1836 Treaty |
| *Michigan v. Bay Mills Indian Community* | 572 U.S. 782 (2014) | Affirmed tribal sovereign immunity — Congress alone can abrogate. | Good law |
| *Kiowa Tribe v. Manufacturing Technologies* | 523 U.S. 751 (1998) | Tribal sovereign immunity applies regardless of where activity occurs. | Good law |
| *Oklahoma v. Castro-Huerta* | 597 U.S. ___ (2022) | Concurrent state *criminal* jurisdiction over non-Indians in Indian Country. Does NOT affect civil jurisdiction. | Active — DOJ consultations on legislative response (Jan-Feb 2025); no legislation enacted as of Feb 2026 |
| *United States v. Cooley* | 141 S. Ct. 1638 (2021) | Tribal officers may detain non-Indians on public highways. | Good law |
| *Flying T Ranch v. Stillaguamish Tribe* | No. 103430-0 (Wash. 2025) | Tribal sovereign immunity bars in rem adverse possession claims over tribally owned non-reservation land. Rejected immovable property exception. | Good law — decided Oct. 9, 2025 |
| *Martorello v. Williams* | Cert. Pet. No. 25-829 | Indian Commerce Clause preemption of state regulation of loans on reservations. | Pending — cert petition filed Jan. 2026 |

## Case Law — AI & Copyright

| Case | Citation | Holding | Status |
|------|----------|---------|--------|
| *Thomson Reuters v. Ross Intelligence* | No. 20-613 (D. Del. 2025, J. Bibas) | Rejected fair use for AI training on copyrighted legal research materials. Third Circuit granted interlocutory appeal (June 2025). | **Appeal pending** — Third Circuit (first appellate AI copyright case) |
| *Bartz v. Anthropic* | No. 24-cv-05417 (N.D. Cal. 2025, J. Alsup) | **Split holding**: (1) Pirated-copy training = "inherently, irredeemably infringing" — not fair use; (2) Lawfully-purchased training = "quintessentially transformative" fair use. $1.5B class settlement (~482,460 works × $3,000). | **Settlement pending** — final fairness hearing April 2026 |
| *Kadrey v. Meta Platforms* | No. 23-cv-03417 (N.D. Cal. 2025, J. Chhabria) | Narrow fair use finding even for pirated copies — but only because plaintiffs failed to show market harm. Judge cautioned "in most cases" training is likely infringing. | Decided June 25, 2025 |
| *International News Service v. Associated Press* | 248 U.S. 215 (1918) | Hot news misappropriation — basis for Theory 6 (common law misappropriation). | Good law — foundational |
